MetricFormulaSubstituted ValuesResultExplanation
Accuracy(TP + TN) / (TP + FP + FN + TN)Percentage of all cases classified correctly.
PrecisionTP / (TP + FP)Of cases predicted as fraud, the proportion that actually were fraud.
RecallTP / (TP + FN)Of actual fraud cases, the proportion correctly identified as fraud.
F1 Score2 × (Precision × Recall) / (Precision + Recall)Harmonic mean of precision and recall; balances the two.

All metrics are shown as percentages to two decimal places. When a denominator is zero, the metric displays N/A instead of dividing by zero. F1 is calculated equivalently as 2 × TP / (2 × TP + FP + FN), avoiding rounding errors.
