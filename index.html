3. Performance Metrics — Excel-Style Calculation Table

MetricResultCalculation FormulaExplanation
Accuracy—=(TP+TN)/(TP+FP+FN+TN)
Share of all transactions classified correctly.
Precision—=TP/(TP+FP)
Of the transactions predicted as fraud, the share that actually were fraud.
Recall—=TP/(TP+FN)
Of all actual fraud transactions, the share the model detected.
F1 Score—=2*TP/(2*TP+FP+FN)
Harmonic mean of precision and recall, balancing false alarms and missed fraud.

Zero-denominator handling: When a metric has no applicable transactions (denominator = 0), its result is shown as N/A rather than displaying an error or an unsupported percentage. F1 uses its equivalent count-based formula.
